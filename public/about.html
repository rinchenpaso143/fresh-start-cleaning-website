About Fresh Start Cleaning Adelaide

Friendly, reliable cleaning for homes and small businesses across Adelaide — built on the same care and attention to detail as a favour for a friend.

Who we are

A small local team, not a call centre

Fresh Start Cleaning Adelaide is a locally owned and operated cleaning service, built around a simple idea: your home or workplace deserves the same care and consistency every single visit — from a team that actually shows up, on time, ready to work.

Whether you need a regular clean to keep on top of things, a deep clean for a fresh reset, or an end-of-lease clean to get your bond back, we bring the same checklist-driven standard to every job, big or small.

Our story

Starting fresh, for you and for us

Fresh Start Cleaning Adelaide began with time spent working in the commercial cleaning industry and seeing, up close, what really makes a difference to clients: turning up reliably, communicating clearly, and never treating a job as "good enough" when it could be done properly.

We started this business to bring that same standard directly to Adelaide homes and small businesses — with upfront pricing, no lock-in contracts on regular cleans, and a genuine guarantee behind every visit.
